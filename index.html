My story

From a business family
to building a media platform.

I grew up around business, studied engineering, and somewhere along the way picked up a camera. What started as photography and casual content turned into something much bigger: a platform built around making Jainism feel present, not distant.

Today that means devotional music through StavansByNamra, long-form conversations through Jain Entrepreneur Stories, and everyday content on faith, youth, and financial literacy — all pointed at the same goal: a community that feels seen by its own generation.
